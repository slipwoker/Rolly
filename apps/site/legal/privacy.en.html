In short

  • Your voice is not recorded. When you talk to Rolly, your voice goes encrypted to our servers, is turned into text and answered in real time. The recording itself is not kept.
  • Conversation transcripts are deleted automatically after 30 days. What Rolly remembers (goals, tasks, summaries) is kept until you delete it, and you can see, correct and delete all of it in the app.
  • You decide what to connect. Google and WhatsApp connect only if you choose to. You can turn off any switch and disconnect any service at any time.
  • Rolly sends nothing on your behalf without your “yes”. Before any email, calendar invitation or WhatsApp message, it reads the draft back and waits for you to confirm.
  • No ads, no selling of data, and no tracking or analytics tools on the website or in the app.
  • Text from conversations and from the services you connect is also processed by OpenAI. Each provider and what it receives is listed below.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Rolly (ROLLY) is a voice companion for your drives. It is operated by Zappy Technologies Ltd. (“Zappy”, “we”), 4 HaShlosha St., Nitzanei Oz, Israel.

This policy covers the website heyrolly.ai and the Rolly app for iPhone and Android. The app is in its last round of testing with a small number of users, and this policy applies to it too.

It follows Israel’s Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (including Amendment 13), the Protection of Privacy (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017, and, for users in the European Union and the UK, the GDPR.

You are under no legal obligation to give us information. Providing it is up to you, but without some of it (for example your voice during a conversation) the service cannot work.

2. What the website collects

  • Waitlist: the email address you enter, the page language (Hebrew or English), and where you came from if known (a utm_source or ref parameter in the link, or the name of the referring site). Your IP address is not stored with the sign-up.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting providers’ servers (Cloudflare and Render) receive your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and keep them in logs for operation and security.
  • Browser storage: the site keeps only two preferences in your browser (localStorage): the language you chose (rolly-lang) and whether you paused animations (rolly-motion). We use no cookies, no analytics and no advertising pixels.
  • Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser details.

3. What the app collects

Device identification

There is no sign-up and no password. On first launch your phone gets a random identifier, stored securely on the phone (in the Keychain) together with the conversation language you chose. We also keep the phone model (for example “iPhone 15”). We do not collect your phone number, email, Apple ID, advertising ID or location.

Your voice

The app uses the microphone only during a conversation you started. Your voice goes encrypted to our servers, is transcribed to text, and Rolly answers out loud. Processing happens in memory, on models we run on dedicated servers, and the recording itself is not stored anywhere.

Conversations and what Rolly remembers

  • Transcripts: the text of what was said in a conversation, by both sides. Saved as the conversation goes (so a dropped connection loses nothing) and deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Rolly’s memory: what you agreed, what matters to you and what is still open: facts and preferences, goals and progress on them, tasks and ideas, a summary of each conversation, a plan for the next ride, and a general description Rolly builds to get to know you. Each item keeps the quote it was learned from.
  • Profile: the name you want Rolly to use and how to pronounce it, the conversation language, and in Hebrew the form of address (masculine, feminine or plural), including the words it was learned from, or what you chose yourself.
  • Other people in the car: what is said in the car may be picked up, including by passengers. When it is unclear who said something, it is kept as “unconfirmed” and deleted automatically after 14 days unless you confirm it. Please let your passengers know Rolly is listening.
  • Sensitive information: we do not ask for sensitive information (such as health data). If you share some in a conversation, it is processed like any other conversation content, and you can delete it at any time.
  • Ride settings: the ride length and conversation language you chose.
  • Quality and usage data: response times, conversation length, technical data, and automatic quality checks of conversations to improve Rolly.
  • Web search: when you ask something that needs current information, the question and the last few lines of the conversation are sent to a search service (OpenAI’s).

Google (only if you connect it)

When you connect your account you allow Rolly to access your calendar (read and create events), read your email and send email. We keep the account’s email address and an encrypted access token. What Rolly does with that access:

  • When you ask: it reads calendar events (title, time and location) and email details (sender, subject, date and a short preview, never the full message).
  • Preparing your next ride: after each conversation Rolly looks at your calendar for the next 14 days (up to 15 events: title, time and place, never descriptions or attendees) and at emails from the last two weeks related to those events (up to 8: sender, subject and preview). From these it prepares at most one topic per source for your next conversation. The content itself is not stored, only the topic it prepared.
  • Actions on your behalf: when you ask, Rolly can send an email from your account or create a calendar event and invite up to 5 people (who get Google’s invitation). To find a recipient’s address it looks in the headers of earlier emails. Every action is read back to you as a draft and happens only after your “yes”, and not at all if it is unclear that you are the one confirming. Rolly never deletes or edits emails or events.

Preparing your next ride is on by default when you connect Google, and you can turn the calendar and the email off separately in the settings (Personalisation → Google). Turning a source off or disconnecting deletes what was prepared from it.

WhatsApp (only if you connect it)

You can connect WhatsApp to Rolly as a “linked device”, like WhatsApp Web, with a pairing code or a link sent to your email. This is an unofficial connection to WhatsApp, and WhatsApp may restrict accounts connected this way. Before connecting you will be asked to accept this explicitly.

  • What is kept: once connected, the messages in all your chats, including groups (text, captions, file names and locations), are kept by us encrypted and deleted automatically 30 days after they reach us, including the history synced when you connect. To show and find chats we also keep chat names and contacts’ names and numbers, which are not encrypted. Voice messages are not kept as audio: they are transcribed to text only when you ask to hear them, and the transcript is kept encrypted for the same period.
  • What Rolly reads: when you ask, one chat or the latest messages you received. In addition, after each conversation it looks at your 5 most active one-to-one chats (not groups) from the last three days, up to 6 text messages from each, to prepare at most one topic for your next conversation. This is also on by default and can be turned off on the WhatsApp screen.
  • Sending: Rolly reads every message back before sending and sends only after your “yes”. A record that a message was sent (without its content) is kept for 48 hours.
  • Other people: your chats contain other people’s messages, and they are kept and processed as described here. Use this connection only if it suits you and the people you message.
  • Disconnecting deletes all the messages, chats and records kept from WhatsApp.

Spotify

For music and podcast search, only the search words are sent to Spotify, with nothing about you. When Rolly shows a song, your phone loads its cover image directly from Spotify, and the play button opens the Spotify app on your phone.

4. How we use information

  • To run the service: hold a voice conversation, remember what you agreed, prepare your next ride, and carry out actions you asked for and confirmed.
  • To improve Rolly: automatic quality checks of conversations and, when something goes wrong or for quality review, a small authorized team may read transcripts.
  • Security and abuse prevention: rate limits, troubleshooting and blocking attacks.
  • Updates: one email to the waitlist when the app is ready.
  • Legal obligations and handling complaints.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or build marketing profiles from it.

For users in the EU and the UK, our legal bases are: performing our agreement with you (running the service), legitimate interests (improvement, security), consent (the waitlist, connecting Google and WhatsApp and preparing your next ride from them, which you can withdraw at any time), and legal obligations.

8. Your rights

Under Israeli law you may access the information about you and ask us to correct or delete information that is not accurate, complete, clear or up to date. Most of this you can do yourself in the app, at any time:

  • See everything Rolly remembers, correct and delete any item.
  • Export your information to a file (WhatsApp messages are not included in the export; you can ask for them by email).
  • Turn off any source used to prepare your next ride (calendar, email, WhatsApp).
  • Disconnect Google (which also revokes the access on Google’s side) or WhatsApp (which deletes all the messages kept).
  • Delete your account and all the information linked to it.

To leave the waitlist, for any other request, or to object to a particular use, email privacy@zappy5.com. We will answer within 30 days. Users in the EU and the UK also have the rights to restrict processing, to data portability and to object, and may complain to their local data protection authority. In Israel you may contact the Privacy Protection Authority at the Ministry of Justice.

9. Security

We protect information under Israel’s Data Security Regulations: all communication is encrypted (TLS), access credentials are stored as hashes and rotated on every use, the identifier on your phone is kept in the Keychain, tokens for outside services and WhatsApp messages are encrypted (AES-256) with separate keys, access to the databases requires authentication over an encrypted connection, server logs do not include conversation content, and access is limited to a small authorized team. No system is completely secure. If a serious security incident happens, we will report it as the law requires.

10. Children

The service is meant for people aged 18 and over, and we do not knowingly collect information about minors. If a minor has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
