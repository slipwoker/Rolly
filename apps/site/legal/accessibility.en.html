At Zappy Technologies Ltd. we want everyone to be able to get to know Rolly and use it, including people with disabilities. Rolly itself is built as a voice service that does not require touching the screen, and we invest in the accessibility of the website too.

1. Accessibility level

The website heyrolly.ai was made accessible under Israel’s Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013. The adjustments follow Israeli Standard SI 5568, which is based on the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0, at level AA.

The latest review was carried out on 26 November 2026 with automated tools (axe) and manual checks: keyboard navigation, heading structure and screen-reader labels, color contrast, text enlarged up to 200%, and phone display.

2. What we did

  • Clear structure: headings in order, defined navigation and content regions, and the language and writing direction set on every page (Hebrew right-to-left, English left-to-right).
  • Keyboard navigation: every link, button and form works with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus indicator and a “Skip to content” link at the top of the page.
  • Screen readers: text descriptions for illustrations, labels for fields and buttons, and form error and success messages that are announced automatically. Decorative elements are hidden from screen readers.
  • Contrast: text and background colors meet the AA contrast ratio, in night mode too.
  • Zoom: text can be enlarged to 200% without losing content, and the site adapts to phones and small screens without sideways scrolling.
  • Motion: a “Pause animations” button at the top of the page stops all motion on the site, and the choice is remembered for your next visit. The site also follows your operating system’s “reduce motion” setting. Nothing on the site flashes.
  • No time limits: nothing on the site has to be done within a time limit.

3. Known limitations

  • The animated conversation at the top of the page is only an illustration and is hidden from screen readers. The text on the page describes the same content.
  • The app itself is still in testing, so this statement covers the website. An accessibility statement for the app will be published when it launches.

If any part of the site is not accessible to you, we would like to know so we can fix it, and we can give you the information another way (for example by email or phone).

4. Physical accessibility

The service is provided online and in the app only. Zappy has no offices or service points open to the public.
